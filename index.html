AI-Driven Product Design & UX Leadership

I drive innovation through AI-enhanced design systems, lead cross-functional design teams, and mentor designers in modern UX practices. My approach combines user research, AI-driven insights, and rigorous design thinking to create scalable solutions.

Expertise & Background

AI & Design: I explore the intersection of artificial intelligence and user-centered design, developing AI-augmented interfaces and design systems that enhance human decision-making and workflow efficiency.

Systems Thinking: I specialize in designing systems that solve complex problems at scale. By combining a background in typography and information design with experience in user-centered design and AI-driven methodologies, I focus on creating coherent, accessible digital ecosystems.

Design Leadership: I graduated with an MA (with distinction) in Information Design and hold a first-class honours degree in Graphic Communication from the world-renowned Department of Typography & Graphic Communication. I was also a finalist at the UXUK awards for my MA project work on behavioral change and sustainable energy consumption.

Core Skills: Product strategy, design systems architecture, AI integration, UX research, information design, design thinking, team leadership, and digital transformation.
